# Premiere Rush's users: what they had, what they need, who's answering them

The research behind the switching page at 1.1 (`../product.md` › scoreboard, the Premiere row; `../plan.md` › Build
order, row 9). Read 2026-11-25 by `video`. Adobe's help pages refuse automated reading (403), so their lines below come
from search snippets of those pages, marked *(snippet)*; Reddit couldn't be read at all, so users' words come from
Adobe's own forums and the App Store.

## In short

- **Rush is gone.** Downloads stopped 2025-09-30. Support ended 2026-09-30, the Creative Cloud app dropped it, and
  installed copies "may no longer function" *(snippet, helpx.adobe.com/premiere-rush/kb/end-of-life.html)*. Its cloud
  sync for personal accounts ended in February 2024, and cloud-only projects can't be recovered (Adobe's Kevin Monahan,
  2026-09-03, community.adobe.com).
- **Adobe sends Rush users to Premiere**: the free phone app (iPhone; Android since 2026-09-22), or Premiere on the
  desktop at $22.99 a month. Rush projects open in desktop Premiere through *Open Rush Project* *(snippet)*.
- **Rush users wanted a simple desktop editor** on a Mac or a Windows PC. They don't want Premiere Pro's interface or its
  price, and they find the phone app too fiddly. **Rush never had automatic captions** (Adobe staff, 2023 and 2024),
  and people asked for them and for silence cutting. hubnx Video's four jobs answer exactly those requests.
- **Nobody ranking for these searches offers a free, local, no-account desktop editor with captions and silence
  removal.** The closest is Mastershot Studio, a free *browser* editor, whose own FAQ has to answer "Does Studio upload
  my videos?".

## What Rush users said (Adobe's forums, the App Store)

- "We can get stuff out easily and quickly then later finetune it." (pseldes, 2025-09-04)
- "I won't use Premiere Pro, for me it is over the top powerful and its interface is insane." (Cary5CA0, 2025-09-04,
  on a MacBook with a 34-inch screen)
- "I want a lightweight video editing programme for use on a Windows PC." (GKN 100, 2025-09-04)
- "I do not use an iPhone and cannot afford to move to Pro." (jamesc15687197, 2025-09-06)
- "I only crop and add captions and currently have to use two different apps." (Beki_Wood4612, 2024-09-08)
- On Premiere for iPhone: "like trying to cut a steak with a butter knife". (squisch, 2025-11-10); "too fiddly", and "I
  would like to open this new software on my desktop pc". (andrewhesselden, 2025-10-16)
- "Canva and Adobe Express are not suitable replacements." (See Chicago Dance, 2026-08-27)
- Some went to CapCut for its ease despite safety worries (2025-09-12); a business kept Rush because it's "safer than
  CapCut" (MightyMoria, 2026-06-26).

Threads: community.adobe.com's Rush end-of-life and discontinuation threads, and *Add auto captions in Premiere Rush
same as CapCut*. The fetch tool summarises pages, so each quote is checked against its thread before it's printed on
the site.

## Who answers these searches now (the search tool's engine, not Google's positions)

- "premiere rush discontinued / replacement / what replaced premiere rush": Adobe's help pages in many regional
  copies, a university IT page and Creative COW.
- "premiere rush alternative": G2, Capterra, SourceForge, AlternativeTo, and vendors' own pages (Blitzcut, slicecut,
  FirstCut, Filmora, Mastershot), plus listicles (FixThePhoto) that never mention the shutdown. **AlternativeTo lists
  only four alternatives and doesn't mark Rush discontinued**: an easy listing at launch.
- Questions their FAQs answer: is Rush discontinued; can I still use it in 2026; what replaces it; what does Adobe
  recommend; is there a free alternative for a computer; can I open my Rush projects elsewhere; does it upload my
  videos.
