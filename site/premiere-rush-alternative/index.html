What happened to Premiere Rush

Adobe stopped offering Premiere Rush for download on September 30, 2025. Support ended on September 30, 2026, and the Creative Cloud app no longer installs it. Adobe says copies already installed may no longer work, and a new computer or system update can stop it at any time.

Adobe points Rush users to Premiere: a free app on iPhone and, since September 2026, Android, or Premiere on the desktop at $22.99 a month.

First, save your Rush projects

  1. If you have Premiere on the desktop, choose Open Rush Project on its start screen. Your edit opens there, though it can't go back to Rush.
  2. If you don't, open each project in Rush while it still starts and export the finished video. Then keep your original clips: any editor opens those, %APP_NAME% included.
  3. Projects kept only in Adobe's cloud stopped syncing in February 2024, and Adobe says deleted cloud copies can't be recovered.
