Privacy

Updated November 25, 2026.

Your videos stay on your computer

%APP_NAME% edits your videos on your own computer. Your videos, their transcripts, your edits and the clean voice it makes are kept there, and go only where you save them. They are never uploaded, and we cannot see them.

No account, tracking or ads

There is nothing to sign up for. The app has no analytics or ads, and this website sets no cookies and runs no tracking scripts.

What the app downloads

The first time you open a video, the app downloads its speech model from Hugging Face and its voice detector from GitHub. The first time you turn on Clean voice, it downloads that model from GitHub. Like any download, those services receive your computer's internet address. After that, the app works without the internet.

Updates

The app checks this website for a newer version of itself when it opens and every six hours while it stays open, and downloads it when there is one. The check sends nothing about you or your videos; like any website, this one sees your computer's internet address.

The one check-in, if you agree

After your first export, the app asks once whether it may send us how it's going. It's off unless you say yes, and you can turn it off in Settings. If you agree, a week after you first opened the app it sends three numbers, once: how many videos you exported, how many minutes it took to your first export, and how many times setting up failed, with the app's version and your system. Never your videos, their words, your name or anything that identifies you or your computer.

Feedback you send

Feedback you send from the app or the feedback page goes to Hub Nexus (hubnx.com), the company that makes %APP_NAME%. It carries what you write, your email if you give one, the app's version and your system, and a picture of the page only if you leave it attached, with anything you blacked out left blank. A problem report from the app adds its log only if you agree. We read it to fix and improve %APP_NAME% and to answer you, and we don't sell or share it. Mail you send to video@hubnx.com we keep to answer you. Ask us there to delete either.

Counting downloads

To know how many people download %APP_NAME%, this website keeps a log of what it serves. For each page or file asked for, it notes the time, which file, whether it was sent and how much of it, and the name of the browser or app that asked. It never notes your internet address or anything else that tells who you are. Each day's log is deleted after 14 days, and we keep only the counts.

Hosting

This website and its downloads are served by Amazon Web Services. Like any web host, it receives your internet address in order to deliver them; the log above leaves it out.
