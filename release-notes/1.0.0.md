The first release of hubnx Video: a free editor for talking videos, on Mac (Apple chip and Intel), Windows and Linux.

## What it does

- **Captions** what you say, word by word or as subtitles, in English and 24 other European languages. Put them in the video, or save them as an .srt file for YouTube.
- **Finds the long pauses** and marks them on the transcript and the timeline. Cut them all with one click, keep the ones you want, and undo anything.
- **Cleans up your voice** with one switch: the hum and the room noise go. Before you turn it on, it says how long it will take.
- **Trim, split and delete**, with unlimited undo. Your edits save as you go.
- **Exports up to 4K** at the quality and loudness YouTube recommends. No watermark.

## Private

No account. Your videos stay on your computer. It sends nothing about you or your videos. It checks for a new version when it opens and every six hours, and downloads its speech model once (about 670 MB); after that, editing works offline. Anything else goes only if you send it: feedback you write, or three counts, once, a week after you start, if you say yes.

## Good to know

- Windows may stop the installer the first time with *Windows protected your PC*. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- In English, possible "um"s are underlined in the transcript. Click one to cut it. They're never cut for you.
- New versions download by themselves and install when you quit.
